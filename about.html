Shifts

A simple personal app for tracking work shifts and calculating hourly pay under Israeli labor rules, with backups to your own Google Drive that you control.

Open the app · Privacy policy

What it does

  • Records work shifts, sick days, days off and holidays for up to three jobs.
  • Calculates pay: overtime at 125% and 150%, Shabbat and holiday rates, sick-day pay, travel, tips and expenses. Every rate is a setting you can change, with history by month.
  • Works as an installable web app (PWA) with a Hebrew, right-to-left interface, and keeps working offline.

Backups (why the app asks for Google Drive access)

Your records are stored on your own device. To protect them from loss, the app can save backup files to a folder named "Shifts Backups" in your own Google Drive, and, when you tap the button, as a downloaded backup file on your phone. For Drive it requests only the drive.file permission, which lets the app see and manage only the files it created itself. It cannot read the rest of your Drive. It also reads your Google account email address, only to show which account is connected.

Backups can be restored from inside the app. See the privacy policy for details on how data is handled.

This is a personal, non-commercial project. There is no server, no advertising and no analytics.
