Privacy policy

Shifts, a personal shift and pay tracker. Last updated: October 9, 2026.

About the app · Open the app

Summary

Your shift records stay on your device and, if you turn backups on, in your own Google Drive and your own phone folder. The developer has no server, receives none of your data, and shows no advertising or analytics.

What data the app handles

  • Shift and pay data that you type in: dates, times, rates, notes, tips, expenses and settings.
  • Your Google account email address, only to show which account is connected for backups.
  • A temporary Google access token that lets the app write backup files while you are signed in.

Where the data is stored

  • On your device, in the browser storage of the installed web app (IndexedDB and local storage). The email address and access token are kept in this same local storage.
  • In backup files that you choose to create: in a folder named "Shifts Backups" in your own Google Drive, and in a folder you pick on your phone, plus any file you download manually.

How Google user data is used

The app requests two Google permissions. The first is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, used only to create, read, verify and remove backup files that the app itself created in your Drive. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive. The second is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, used only to display the connected account. Google user data is used for these purposes only. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not read by any person.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The only network requests made by the app go directly from your device to Google (sign-in and Drive) and to the host that serves the app files. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and receives the standard web server request information (such as your IP address) that any website receives when you load the app.

Retention and deletion

  • The app keeps up to 10 backup copies and removes older ones after a new copy is verified (in Google Drive, removed files go to the Drive trash).
  • You can delete the backups at any time by deleting the "Shifts Backups" folder in Google Drive, or the files in your phone folder.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clearing the site data or uninstalling the app removes the data stored on the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. For questions, contact the owner of the GitHub account that hosts this site (the account name is the first part of this page's address).
